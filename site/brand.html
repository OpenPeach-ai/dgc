Clear space

Leave at least one full mark-width around the symbol. At small sizes, use the mark alone; keep the wordmark horizontal and do not reconstruct its spacing.

Do

  • Use violet for DGC marks and measured DGC data.
  • Use the mono mark when colour is unavailable.
  • Preserve the original SVG paths and proportions.

Do not

  • Do not rotate, outline, stretch, or add gradients to the mark.
  • Do not recolour competitor data violet.
  • Do not use “Dagucci Code” or introduce another product name.
